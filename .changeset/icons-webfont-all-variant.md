Restored the webfont variant that contains both outline and filled icons. `tabler-icons.css` (and `tabler-icons-200.css` / `tabler-icons-300.css`) once again includes filled icons with the `-filled` suffix (e.g. `ti-circle-x-filled`), as before 3.36.0. Outline-only builds are now available as `tabler-icons-outline.css`, `tabler-icons-200-outline.css` and `tabler-icons-300-outline.css`.
